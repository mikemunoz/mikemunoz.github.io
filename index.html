About

I'm Mike Munoz, Technical Client Director at Ark Technology Consultants. I help enterprise, mid-market, healthcare and manufacturing organizations design and deliver networking, security, cloud and data center solutions built on Cisco technologies.

Focus Areas

Architecture across the full infrastructure stack, from the campus edge to the data center and cloud.

Enterprise Networking

Campus, branch, WAN and SD-WAN designs built for availability, scale and simpler operations.

Security

Firewall, segmentation, identity and secure access architectures aligned to risk and compliance.

Cloud

Hybrid and multi-cloud connectivity, security and operations that extend the on-prem estate cleanly.

Data Center

Fabric, compute and storage networking designs that support modernization and growth.

How I Help

I work with technical and business stakeholders to turn requirements into designs that can be scoped, priced and delivered with confidence.

Solution Design

Discovery, current-state assessment and target architectures that map to business outcomes.

Proposals and SOWs

Clear scope, assumptions and deliverables so projects start aligned and finish on plan.

RFP and RFI Responses

Technically sound, compliant responses that show how the solution meets each requirement.

Technical Leadership

A trusted advisor from first conversation through handoff to delivery.
